    About this register

    This register contains a record of notified acquisitions and applications for notification waivers. For notified acquisitions, entries include details of any public consultation being undertaken.

    Notified acquisition entries are generally created within one business day of receiving the notification. The ACCC’s determination and reasons are also published within one business day after a decision is made.

    Notification waiver application entries are published after a notification waiver decision is made, usually within one business day. 

    Learn more about the merger control regime and notifying an acquisition.

    For questions about:

    • a notified acquisition, email mergers@accc.gov.au
    • a notification waiver application, email notificationwaivers@accc.gov.au.

    Include the title of the acquisition in the subject line.

    If you have a general question about merger reform or the merger control regime, email MergerReformInfo@accc.gov.au.

    Seagull is majority-owned and controlled by funds managed by Peak Rock Capital (Peak Rock).

    Seagull develops and supplies software used to design, manage and automate labels, barcodes, Radio-Frequency Identification tags and related types of identification (labelling software) and tracking processes (track and trace software). Seagull's principal product is its BarTender platform. Its labelling software is primarily provided as an 'on-premises solution', supplied indirectly via distributors and is targeted at businesses with less complex requirements.

    Loftware also develops and supplies labelling software but does not supply track and trace services, including in Australia. Loftware's labelling software is primarily supplied as a cloud-based solution, which is targeted at larger companies with more complex requirements.

    AKKR is a technology-focused private equity investor with investments in a range of software and technology businesses. Through its portfolio companies, AKKR has interests in a range of differentiated businesses that supply software to business customers across a variety of industries.

    Riverside is a private equity investor that focuses on investments in founder-owned healthcare companies in the lower middle market.
